## 📌 Business Problem
To evaluate BMW's 15-year global sales trajectory (2010–2024), examining long-term market trends, regional vehicle demand, revenue generation across model lines, and strategic shifts in automotive retail.

## 📊 Key Insights & Highlights
* **Longitudinal Sales Trends:** Mapped a 15-year sales performance curve to track growth cycles, market recoveries, and overall brand momentum.
* **Model & Regional Breakdown:** Identified top-performing vehicle series and geographical regions driving core profitability.
* **Strategic Automotive Retail Insights:** Derived data-backed recommendations on demand forecasting and regional inventory allocation for global markets.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills
* **Tools:** Data Analytics, Data Visualization, Excel / Power BI / SQL
* **Key Skills:** Longitudinal Trend Analysis, Automotive Retail Strategy, Market Performance Tracking, Business Intelligence
